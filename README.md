# Radio Free Razorwind

Bring standard-issue Goblin engineering and classic radio vibes directly to your World of Warcraft UI! Radio Free Razorwind is a lightweight, immersive ambient music player and simulated radio station that plays back-to-back track selections from World of Warcraft's iconic soundtrack—complete with voiceover station announcements and customizable sponsor commercials.

Features

Retro LCD Display Interface: Features a custom, draggable retro-style chassis complete with a dynamic green LCD screen showing track titles and precise elapsed playback timers.

Continuous Playback Engine: Seamlessly cycles through built-in music tracks, automatically starting new songs as current ones end.

TTS Station DJs & Commercial Breakdown: Integrated Text-to-Speech brings your station to life with station IDs and fictional sponsor advertisements (featuring Kaja'Cola, Smokywood Pastures, and Crown Chemical Co.).

Customizable Commercial Frequency: Tailor your listening experience! Set sponsor ads to play every 1 to 5 songs, or disable them entirely for non-stop music.

System Voice Selector: Easily pick your preferred TTS voice for station announcements right from the UI using the built-in dropdown menu.

Full Audio Control: Adjust background music volume directly on the radio chassis using the integrated volume slider.

How to Use

Load into the game with the addon enabled.

Open the radio interface by typing /rfr or /razorwind into your chat box.

Click POWER to start the broadcast, or use TUNE to instantly skip to a random track.

Use the dropdown menus on the control panel to customize your TTS Voice and Commercial Frequency on the fly!

Slash Commands

/rfr — Toggle the Radio Free Razorwind interface.

/razorwind — Alternate command to toggle the radio interface.
